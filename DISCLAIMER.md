# Educational Use Only

This repository is an **educational demonstration**. It exists to show how a real
browser can be driven with human-like behaviour and how pages can be parsed into
structured data. It is not a product, a service, or a tool for production use.

## Scraping and Google's Terms of Service

Automated access to Google Search **violates Google's Terms of Service**. Google
explicitly prohibits scraping, automated querying, and any attempt to bypass the
technical and security mechanisms it uses to enforce those terms.

By the same token, **bypassing or defeating security mechanisms is prohibited.**
That includes, but is not limited to:

- circumventing bot detection, CAPTCHAs, rate limits, or access controls;
- ignoring `robots.txt`, Terms of Service, or other access restrictions;
- disguising automated traffic in order to evade detection.

Those activities are unlawful in many jurisdictions (for example under
computer-misuse statutes) and are contrary to the intent of this project.

## What this project is for

- Learning about browser automation, human-like interaction, and HTML parsing.
- Studying the design of a small, layered Python application.
- Experimenting **only** with data and sites you own or are explicitly authorised
  to access, and only where their terms permit automated access.

## What this project is not for

- Scraping Google or any service whose terms prohibit it.
- Bypassing anti-bot or other security controls.
- Building a dataset of personal or business contact information without consent.
- Any commercial, surveillance, or privacy-invasive use.
